# Shokupan cat behavior references

Status: **Exploration**

This library records pose and expression research for original Shokupan-cat
art. Links are visual references, not assets approved for redistribution.

## Usage and rights boundary

- Extract general pose, timing, camera, and expression ideas only.
- Do not copy, trace, bundle, or redistribute the source images.
- Do not preserve captions, backgrounds, famous meme identities, or a source
  photo's exact composition.
- Production assets must be original project art with explicit authorship and
  licensing metadata.
- A public image URL does not imply permission to reuse the underlying image.

## Initial cycling-idle roster

The pet should always have a nearly static breathing loop. At long, irregular
intervals it may leave that loop for one short action, then settle back into the
same anchored pose.

1. Slow blink
2. Ear twitch
3. Tail-tip curl
4. Paw over face and resettle
5. Two or three kneading presses
6. Forward stretch
7. Paw grooming and forehead wipe
8. Rare tiny blep

Avoid playing two large silhouette-changing actions consecutively. Reduced
motion uses a still pose plus slow blink only.

## Focus-state candidates

- **Focused:** breathing loop plus the cycling-idle roster.
- **Grace:** one eye opens, ear turn, or small head tilt.
- **Nudge:** corner peek, gentle paw tap, side-eye, or `0.5x` stare.
- **Intervention:** crossed-paw wait or skeptical squint; exact direction is
  not locked.
- **Returned:** blink, brief blep, or compact resettle.
- **Completed:** long stretch or belly-up flop; exact direction is not locked.
